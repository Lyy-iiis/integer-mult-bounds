# Uniform endpoint obstruction for the mirrored five-query pair

Scope: one fixed finite tensor-word program for every positive width f, with fixed scalar coefficients and free operations in the span of tensor powers of the finite monomial group H. Here H is enlarged to all 8-by-8 permutation matrices with independent fourth-root entry phases, containing the permitted free group. A lower bound with this enlargement also holds for the original smaller group. This is not a statement about one fixed-width matrix program, or a width-dependent family of unrelated scalar coefficients/decoders.

Let A=C[H], G the actual base matrix group generated by free and paid letters. Do not identify central phases. Regard a register on two independent inputs as a row in C[G]^2, with free operations acting by left multiplication by A. Each new paid query contributes at most dim(A) to the complex dimension of the accessible left-A span, even when its input mixes all retained states.

The mirror state has exact individual-word reductions

s = K a x + D_A y,
t = D_B x + K b y,
D_A = M_A C2 + (1/4) JA P2 V02 C0 + (1/2) JA Q2 V02 C0,
D_B = M_B C1 + (1/4) JB P1 V01 C0 + (1/2) JB Q1 V01 C0.

Here a,b,M_A,M_B are free monomials. The other retained states are x,y,u=C0x,v=C0y,r=A01x+A02y, with A01=C0 P01 C0 and A02=C0 P02 C0.

Project onto the four left-coset/input coordinates

(x,HK), (y,HK), (y,HC2), (x,HC1).

This projection is A-linear. The exact base-matrix check COSET.json establishes:

- x,y,u,v,r have zero projection;
- K has eight nonzeros per column; C0,C1,C2 have two, and HC0,HC1,HC2 are distinct;
- A01,A02 and the two additional terms in each residual have four nonzeros per column, hence they lie in neither HK nor the one-call cosets;
- the leading residual terms are exactly M_A C2 and M_B C1.

In the displayed coordinates, s and t are therefore

(Ka, 0, M_A C2, 0),
(0, Kb, 0, M_B C1).

Every displayed nonzero coefficient is a free-monomial unit after choosing a coset representative. Consequently the old projected span is a graph of dimension 2 dim(A), with zero intersection with the target-only subspace spanned by Kx and Ky. Adding the two target rows to the old span increases dimension by 2 dim(A). A single fresh query can increase it by at most dim(A), so at least TWO additional queries are necessary, regardless of their chronology or free mixing.

For k independent disjoint copies, use the same four coordinates for every pair. The direct-sum graph has dimension 2k dim(A), and adding all 2k targets raises dimension by 2k dim(A). At least 2k further query outputs are necessary. In particular, three completed copies of this five-query preparation cannot be extended to six targets in seventeen total queries: at least twenty-one queries are required for this frozen preparation. This does not exclude changing the earlier five-query words or mixing inputs before the preparation is complete.

Uniform tensor-word faithfulness justifies the formal group-algebra argument: for a finite set of distinct nonzero base matrices g, choose a linear functional ell with distinct nonzero values ell(g). If sum_g c_g g^{tensor f}=0 for every positive integer f, apply ell^{tensor f} for f=1,...,n. The resulting Vandermonde system is invertible, so all c_g vanish. Apply this separately to each independent input coefficient. This retains scalar phases and does not assume G is finite; only H is finite for the dimension estimate.

The hook checks are deliberately weaker. The mirror has exact width-five stage ranks 80,160,240,320,400 and target joint448 on all eight cuts, and the natural width-six modular screen attains 640,1280,1920,2560,3200 and joint3584. Many simple sixth queries even close the width-five hook deficit. None of these facts invalidates the uniform left-module obstruction.

Constructive lesson: the useful phase/shared-call idea must be revised so the lower-depth residual coordinates are already represented by retained anchors, or are shared nontrivially across inputs before the high-depth target rows are seeded. Independent full-rank one-call residual anchors on every target create a hidden uniform endpoint debt that hook saturation does not detect.
